About me

Hi, I'm Murtaza. I build the systems that make AI agents reliable.

I'm an AI Engineer at Codet.ai in Karachi, where I work on the parts of an AI platform most people never see: the knowledge graph agents reason over, the search that finds the right facts, the warehouse that turns raw product events into metrics, and the voice and messaging pipelines that let agents talk to real customers.

I studied computer science at FAST-NUCES and started in data engineering, building Spark pipelines at Systems Limited and SAP planning models at TallyMarks Consulting. That data foundation shapes how I build AI today: start from the data model, make every number trustworthy, then put intelligence on top. I also guide engineers on backend design, and I like owning a product end to end, from requirements to production.
